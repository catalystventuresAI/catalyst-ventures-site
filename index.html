ROB GOEHRING FOUNDER · CEO

Rob Goehring.
Entrepreneur, strategist, ecosystem builder.

Rob is a serial entrepreneur, AI strategist, ecosystem builder, and executive advisor with more than 25 years of experience across software, SaaS, fintech, cybersecurity, and artificial intelligence.

He is the founder and CEO of Wisr AI, Executive Director of the AI Network of BC, and Founding Director of the AI Chief Executive Council at BC Tech Association.

Rob works with organizations across industry, government, and the innovation ecosystem to accelerate AI adoption, commercialization, ecosystem growth, and strategic transformation.
